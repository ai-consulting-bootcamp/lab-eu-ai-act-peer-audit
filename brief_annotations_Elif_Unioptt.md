**Source:** Elif's Phase 1 system brief, received 17 November 2026. No other documentation was exchanged.

**Legend** (Markdown can't underline or circle, so I use tags):
- **[T]** affects the risk-tier classification ("underline")
- **[?]** unclear, needs clarification before forming a view ("circle")

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "checks the facts about one university programme and writes a report for a Turkish student and their family" | [T] [?] | Education domain, so Annex III point 3 is in play. *Turkish* students: where are they, and where is Unioptt established? This decides whether the AI Act applies at all (Art. 2). |
| 2 | "Plain computer rules (not the AI) then decide whether each fact counts as Verified, Needs Review, Inferred or Unknown" | [T] | The LLM only extracts; code assigns status. Supports a "narrow procedural / preparatory task" reading (Art. 6(3)(a)/(d)). |
| 3 | "compares the requirements with the student's profile" → "met / gap / check" items | [T] [O] | The key element. It evaluates a person's grades, language level and budget against entry requirements. That is close to Annex III 3(c), *"assessing the appropriate level of education an individual … will be able to access"*, and arguably **profiling**, which blocks the Art. 6(3) exemption. |
| 4 | "student profile: grades, language test scores, budget, citizenship … often a minor's" | [T] [O] [?] | Minors = vulnerable group (relevant to Art. 5(1)(b) and to deployer care). Clear GDPR/KVKK obligations: parental consent, minimisation. Who enters the data? |
| 5 | "It does not give a score, a ranking, an admission prediction or a decision" | [T] | Strong design choice against high-risk. But "gap" items can steer a family's decision as effectively as a score. |
| 6 | "may decide whether to apply, or how to spend money and time" | [T] | Real-world effect on access to education, even if indirect. |
| 7 | "In the MVP, no one reviews the report before the student sees it" | [O] [?] | No human oversight. Fine for minimal risk; a gap if high-risk (Art. 14). Is review planned for production? |
| 8 | "the report is assembled by code, not written by the AI" | [T] [?] | Lowers Art. 50(2) relevance (synthetic text). But are "next steps" generated by the LLM? |
| 9 | "OpenAI (a general-purpose language model)" | [O] | OpenAI = GPAI model provider (Art. 53 duties sit with OpenAI). Elif = downstream provider of an AI system. |

Schools become deployers. |
