# Stretch – Consulting work plan for Finding 1
**Finding 1 – Classification depends on an undefined business model (Annex III 4(a), Art. 6(3))** · Significant (Blocking if B2B)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Deliverables that close the gap** | (1) **Intended-purpose statement**, one page: who uses the system, for what, and explicitly excluded uses (employer screening, ranking applicants, public-employment-service eligibility). (2) **Terms of service clause** prohibiting use for recruitment or selection decisions. (3) **Product change:** visible "personal use only – not for selection decisions" label on UI and all exports, and no employer-facing API or sharing feature. (4) **Legal classification memo** confirming Annex III 4(a) does not apply to the B2C purpose. |
| **Responsible** | Product owner (builder): statement and product change. Lawyer: memo and terms review. Consultant (auditor): draft statement, misuse scenarios, evidence checklist. |
| **Realistic timeline** | Week 1: draft intended-purpose statement and misuse scenarios. Week 2: product changes (labels, export notice) and terms draft. Weeks 3–4: legal review and memo. Before any B2B sales talk: mandatory reassessment gate. |
| **Evidence that the gap is closed** | Signed intended-purpose statement in the technical file. Live terms with the exclusion clause. Screenshots or exports showing the label. Code review confirming no employer-facing endpoint. Lawyer's memo. Entry in the product decision log that any B2B channel triggers a new AI Act assessment. |
