# Teammate System Brief

**Teammate:** Armin  
**Selected scenario:** Case 1

An industrial equipment trading company spends substantial time reviewing procurement documents. Project experience is not consistently preserved for future use. It wants to purchase software to support quotation preparation. Compare customer inquiries with supplier quotations and approved historical records; flag discrepancies; draft technical and commercial proposals, including new explanations of advantages, technical suitability, and reasons for selecting a supplier. Technical claims should reference supporting documents. Customer specifications and inquiries; supplier technical documents, datasheets, and financial quotations; approved transaction records, reviewed emails, and approved expert notes. Commercial and technical staff use the output. Errors can cause financial losses for the company and its customers, and unsuitable equipment may create safety hazards for customers. An expert checks the draft against customer requirements and the selected supplier's technical and commercial documents, confirms the supplier and final price, and sends the proposal. Sending stops if an essential technical claim lacks reliable evidence or a discrepancy remains unresolved. The proposal goes to a specific customer in a private transaction.
