## Input

The input was the Case 1 system brief supplied by my teammate. I reviewed only the system brief and did not inspect his risk classification, gap analysis, or compliance memo before completing my independent assessment.

## Audit workflow

1. Read the brief without reviewing the builder's conclusions.
2. Identified facts affecting Article 5, Article 6 and Annex III, and Article 50.
3. Recorded five clarification questions and provisional assumptions.
4. Mapped the likely provider, deployer, model provider, technical vendors, and human reviewer.
5. Classified the system and documented five findings with severity, action, and escalation.
6. Prepared an overall recommendation and a debrief template.

## Evidence produced

The classification table, role map, findings, and recommendations are documented in [audit-report.md](audit-report.md). The information requests and assumptions are documented in [clarifying-questions.md](clarifying-questions.md).

## Final output

The final provisional output is an independent audit classifying the system as **limited risk / transparency obligations**, because Article 50(2) may apply to its generated proposal text. The recommendation is **proceed with conditions**.

## Failure or limitation

The audit was based only on a short written brief. No architecture diagram, vendor contract, data-flow map, technical test result, or workflow export was available. The identity of the provider and the implementation of output marking, data controls, logging, and mandatory approval therefore remain unverified. The closing note must be finalised after the debrief with the teammate.
